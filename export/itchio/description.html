🐑 Save the Sheep

An arcade action game where you keep the sheep alive to finish every level, alone or with a friend.

👥 New: 2 players co-op on the same screen!

Grab a friend, a sibling or your kid: pick 2 PLAYERS in the menu and save the sheep together through the whole adventure, bonus level included.

Gordon has one job: protect the sheep. The sheep walks on its own, and Gordon has to clear the way so it can reach the end of each level in one piece.

If the sheep doesn't make it, neither do you! 🐾

🤝 Play together

  • 🎮 Any setup: two players on one keyboard, a keyboard + a gamepad, or two gamepads. A gamepad plugged in? Player 2 takes it.
  • ⌨️ Your keys, your way: every key and gamepad button can be changed before the game starts, and the setup is remembered for next time
  • 🧪 Try before you play: each player tests their controls with their own Gordon in the TEST area, then START
  • 🚪 Teamwork required: gates and elevators only move once both players pull the handle
  • ❤️ Two life bars: each player has their own life bar, the sheep's life is shared
  • 💫 Never out for long: a fallen partner is back at the next level
  • 🏍️ Two motorbikes, two roads: in the bonus level each player rides their own bike
  • 🐺 Two Gordons: P1 in blue, P2 in orange, with tags above their heads

✨ Features

  • 🌃 A city at night: run and jump across rooftops, streets and building sites under a starry sky
  • 💦 Water gun: blast the enemies (dinosaurs included!) before they reach the sheep
  • 🚪 Action handle: open gates to let the sheep through
  • 🛗 Elevators: turn them on to carry the sheep over obstacles
  • 🏍️ Bonus level: ride the motorbike, jump the rocks and grab water and desserts before time runs out
  • 👥 Solo or 2 players co-op on the same screen, on one keyboard or with gamepads
  • 👶 For everyone: suitable from age 3

🎮 Controls

1 player

  • ← →: move
  • Space / Z: jump
  • Enter / A: shoot
  • Tab / E: action (gates, elevators)
  • Esc: pause
  • M: sound on / off

2 players (shared keyboard, default keys)

Player 1Player 2
MoveQ D (A D on QWERTY)← →
JumpZ (W on QWERTY)↑
ShootFL
ActionGK

After 1 PLAYER or 2 PLAYERS in the menu, each player picks keyboard or gamepad, can change their keys and try them in the test area before START.

🕹️ Gamepads are supported too, and touch screens get on-screen controls.
